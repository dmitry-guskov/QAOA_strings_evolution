# Read-only audit of the tied-angle truncation prototype

Facts: Audited source SHA256 is 4f4c4a82778583f7d9e0f4dfffd1141d6f116d2f512047c3e801a6c474ffb0b6, the archived pilot-v1 implementation. A stable copy, exact selected pilot inputs, executable probes, raw JSON results and hashes are retained in this directory. The audit was run against an isolated archived source copy. The parent subsequently repaired the public suffix helper's handling of parallel edges; the archived counterexample is intentionally retained as evidence for the repair.

Correctness assessment: I found no truncation-certificate error in propagate_with_bounds on its supported graph inputs. Mixer rotations preserve z masks; cost rotations add only incidence vectors of remaining edges, so the connected-component parity filter proves an identically zero final expectation and gradient when it rejects. It does not assume a small coefficient. The fixed Pauli-weight mask is parameter-independent. The Hermitian product sign, pointwise product-rule jets, and interval product-rule jets correctly accumulate repeated occurrences of the same gamma or beta. Under the constructed reverse chronological gate order, an all-ZZ remaining suffix is in chronological layer zero, so g[0], radii[0], and the first gamma derivative slot are correct. Empty suffixes and p=0 remain consistent. Independent dense-state and box probes support this assessment; they do not establish machine-rounding certification.

Validation: probe.py runs 54 arbitrary-Hermitian-observable dense-state comparisons and 270 box-point comparisons across n=2 through 5, p=0 through 3, signed, disconnected and edgeless graphs, including Clifford centers. Maximum full-energy discrepancy was 9.44e-16; full-gradient discrepancy 7.11e-15; box-gradient enclosure residual 1.14e-14; surrogate finite-difference discrepancy 3.71e-10. The statevector is constructed directly from diagonal cost phases and single-qubit mixer amplitudes, independently of Pauli propagation.

Public-helper defect found in archived v1: suffix_finner_bound(2,2,3,((0,1,1.),(0,1,1.)),gamma=.2) incorrectly treats repeated occurrences of one neighbor as independent spin variables. For P=YZ, the exact amplitude is sin(.8)=.71735609 but the returned cap is .50724736; exact derivative is 2.78682684 but the cap is 1.97058415. The main API always generates unique graph edges and was unaffected. Combining parallel weights or rejecting repeated unordered edges is required. The parent reports and implemented the combination repair.

## What the diagnostics establish

Facts: In optimized p=4, weight-cutoff 4, point-box pilot cases, the final-ZZ contribution is a very small part of the remaining bound. On the prism it is .03206 out of 2.78936; on Petersen it is .01888 out of 1.5380; on the 12-vertex cubic instance it is .01656 out of 3.8875. Perfecting the pure-final-suffix calculation cannot fix the dominant earlier contributions on these cases.

A pointwise exact discarded-batch operator norm is a strict diagnostic of what any state-independent norm improvement at that same discard time could achieve. group_diagnostic.py reconstructs each complete merged dropped batch, forms its 64-by-64 Hermitian matrix, and sums its exact spectral norm. For optimized K3,3 p=4/cutoff4, the early-stage l1 sum 5.83383 becomes 2.87814 under a greedy anticommuting partition and 1.69885 under the exact spectral norm. On the prism the corresponding values are 2.75730, 1.46471 and 1.01913. Thus even the ideal pointwise operator norm for each discarded batch leaves the early-stage bound too large for a .01 energy target on these selected cases. This conclusion is specific to the per-stage triangle-sum family, not to all possible norm-based or reorganized certificates.

scalar_diagnostic.py computes the exact state-aware contribution of each discarded batch by propagating the entire remaining circuit as a dense state. It then takes the absolute value only after summing the batch's signed Pauli contributions. The signed contributions reconstruct the actual truncation error to at most 5.56e-16. This is an expensive oracle diagnostic, not a scalable proposed simulator.

For K3,3 p=2/cutoff4, the state-aware batch absolute sum is .046583 versus actual error .044907 and the implemented suffix certificate .716323. For the prism p=2/cutoff4, those numbers are .021904, .016819 and .359806. For K3,3 p=4/cutoff4, they are .245454, .244574 and 5.856049. For the prism p=4/cutoff4, they are .124978, .009449 and 2.789360. The cycle6 p=2/cutoff3 has exactly zero measured truncation error and a state-aware batch absolute sum at numerical zero, while the implemented certificate is .333333. Therefore state awareness matters strongly, and the prism p=4 example additionally requires substantial cancellation between different discard times to certify its observed error near .01 using this decomposition.

Recommendations: The one stronger experiment worth pursuing is bounded continuation and signed aggregation of discarded residuals across a complete QAOA layer, before converting the residual into a norm or a scalar absolute-value bound. The oracle diagnostic identifies why: same-stage operator-norm improvements alone remain large, and for the prism p4 even exact same-stage scalar magnitudes retain a roughly 13-fold gap. Any such continuation must charge its extra propagation and memory cost and certify any secondary residual truncation. Do not present this as established efficient or novel. A useful kill criterion is failure to reach a preset .01 energy target at a total cost below increasing the retained Pauli cutoff or direct exact/lightcone simulation on the same instances.

Narrow negative claim: For the recorded optimized centers on these specific small graphs, reverse chronological single-gate truncation with a fixed Pauli-weight cutoff4 and the cumulative discarded-coefficient l1 certificate, tightened only at the terminal commuting-ZZ suffix by component parity and Finner moment bounds, generally fails to certify .01 energy accuracy despite much smaller observed error on some instances. On the selected K3,3 and prism p4 cases, replacing each earlier dropped batch's l1 by its exact operator norm still does not meet that target. These are point-box results, so they also lower-bound the difficulty for a uniform box containing the same center within this bound family. This does not prove failure of a different mask, ordering, error decomposition, state-aware certificate, graph family, depth, or parameter regime.

## Reproduction

Use the available numerical runtime: python3 -B QAOA_strings_evolution/research/diagnostics/audit/probe.py . Then run group_diagnostic.py and scalar_diagnostic.py with the same interpreter and -B option. Scripts are pinned to the archived source copy and pilot_inputs.json here, so later repository changes do not change their workload. Each writes its named JSON result locally. NumPy used in this audit was 2.3.5. The companion provenance.json records source, scripts, input and raw-result hashes; floating timings can change on rerun.
